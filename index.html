Your family's plan for when things go wrong.

After an earthquake, a fire or a long blackout, calls fail and families lose each other. MeetPoint keeps your family's plan on every phone — where to meet, who does what — and sends “I'm OK” every way it can at the same time: text message, internet, phones nearby and Meshtastic® radio. More ways mean more chances that your news gets through.

  • 🔒 Encrypted with your family's key
  • 👤 No account, no sign-up
  • 🚫 No ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • 🙈 Not even we can see your plan or messages

One plan for the family

Meeting points near home, out of the neighbourhood and out of town, who picks up whom, and the notes that matter on the day.

“I'm OK” or “Help”

One tap. It goes out on every network at once and keeps trying on each one until everyone in the family has it or a way opens up.

Four ways through

Text message, internet, phone to phone over Bluetooth with no network at all, and Meshtastic LoRa radio for kilometres. Each needs something to work — a signal, a phone in range, a radio within reach — so none is a promise; together they give you more chances.

Phones carry messages

A family phone keeps your news and passes it on to the next family phone it meets, for up to a week.

Map that works offline

The streets around your meeting points are saved on the phone, with where everyone was last and how far each point is.

iPhone and Android

The whole family on one plan, whatever phone they have. Only your family can read your messages.

Your privacy

  • Encrypted on your phone. Every “I'm OK”, position and plan update is encrypted before it leaves, with a key only your family's phones have.
  • No account. No email, no sign-up.
  • On your family's phones only. The plan, the messages and the log live on your phones. Delete the app and they're gone.
  • Sealed on the way. The relay, the radios and other families' phones that pass messages on carry them sealed: they can't open them, and neither can we.
  • Nothing watching. No ads, no analytics, no trackers, in the app or on this site.

Two exceptions you choose yourself: a text message (SMS), like any SMS, isn't encrypted; nor is the radio's Also Send as Plain Text copy, off unless you turn it on. Read the full privacy policy →
