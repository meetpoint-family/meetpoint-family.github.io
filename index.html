Your family's plan for when things go wrong.

After an earthquake, a fire or a long blackout, calls fail and families lose each other. MeetPoint keeps your family's plan on every phone — where to meet, who does what — and gets “I'm OK” through by whatever still works: text message, internet, phones nearby or Meshtastic radio.

One plan for the family

Meeting points near home, out of the neighbourhood and out of town, who picks up whom, and the notes that matter on the day.

“I'm OK” or “Help”

One tap. It goes out on every network at once and keeps trying until everyone in the family has it.

Four ways through

Text message, internet, phone to phone over Bluetooth with no network at all, and Meshtastic LoRa radio for kilometres.

Phones carry messages

A family phone keeps your news and passes it on to the next family phone it meets, for up to a week.

Map that works offline

The streets around your meeting points are saved on the phone, with where everyone was last and how far each point is.

iPhone and Android

The whole family on one plan, whatever phone they have. Only your family can read your messages.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your plan stays on your family's phones, and every message is sealed with your family's key before it leaves the phone: the relay server and the phones that carry it can't read it.
