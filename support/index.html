Common questions

My family shows Offline

Their phone hasn't been heard for a few minutes: MeetPoint was closed (swiped away), the phone is off, or it has no network and no family phone nearby. On iPhone, location must be set to Always, and on Android MeetPoint's notification must stay on, so it keeps running in the background.

The Internet icon stays yellow

The phone has Wi-Fi or mobile data but can't reach the relay server. Check that everyone uses the same relay (⚙︎ → Internet Relay) and that mobile data is on for MeetPoint.

Phones nearby don't find each other

Bluetooth must be on, and both phones need MeetPoint running. Come within 10–50 m. Two iPhones also meet over Wi-Fi; an iPhone and an Android phone need Bluetooth.

Move to a new phone

Install MeetPoint on the new phone and join your family again with the invite from someone in it (⚙︎ → Invite Family).
