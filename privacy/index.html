Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · applies to MeetPoint for iPhone and Android and to this website

MeetPoint is made by Miguel Gonçalves. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: the developer collects no personal data. Your plan, your position and your messages go only to your own family, sealed with a key that only your family's phones have.

Data we collect

None. MeetPoint has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer can't see your plan, your family, your position or your messages.

Your family's plan

The plan — meeting points, names, phone numbers, tasks and notes — is stored on the phones of your family. It goes from one phone to another only when you invite someone (QR code or link) or when the plan is updated. Whoever has the invite can join and read the family's messages, so share it only with your family.

Your location

MeetPoint uses your location to show how far the meeting points are, to tell your family when you reach a meeting point, and to send your position to your family about every 15 minutes when you move, also while the app is in the background. It is sent only to the members of your family, sealed with your family's key. It also finds which country you are in, on the phone, to show the right emergency number.

How messages travel

Every message MeetPoint sends (“I'm OK”, “Help”, positions, confirmations, plan updates) is encrypted on your phone with your family's key (AES-GCM). It can then travel four ways:

  • Internet relay. Sealed messages go through an ntfy relay server — by default the developer's NeedToApp server (meetpoint.relay.needtoapp.com), hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany (EU), or one you choose in the settings. Until every family phone has MeetPoint 1.0.1, messages also go through the public ntfy.sh server (run by a third party), so phones still on 1.0 keep in touch. The servers can't read the messages or tell who is talking; they see the sealed message, a topic name worked out from your family's key, the time, and the IP address of the phone that connects. The NeedToApp server keeps messages for at most 48 hours (ntfy.sh: 12 hours) so phones that were offline catch up, then deletes them; its server logs are used only to keep the service running and are deleted after at most 7 days.
  • Phones nearby. Over Bluetooth (and Wi-Fi between iPhones), sealed messages pass to other phones running MeetPoint. Phones of other families may carry your sealed messages to pass them on, without being able to read them, and drop them when they expire (at most 7 days).
  • Meshtastic® radio. If you link a radio, sealed messages go out over LoRa. If you turn on Also Send as Plain Text (off by default), a short readable copy with your name and position is sent too, and anyone on that radio channel can read it.
  • Text message (SMS). Only when you tap Send My Situation by SMS, or confirm a family member's message by SMS. A text message is not encrypted: it contains your name, how you are and where you are, and goes through your mobile operator like any SMS. Your messages app sends it, and MeetPoint never reads your text messages: you open a family message by tapping its link. (A separate Android version installed from outside Google Play can, with your permission, send texts itself and read incoming texts only to find MeetPoint links from your family.)
  • MeetPoint links. Links in texts and invites point to this website (/m/#…, /join/#…), so any messages app can open them. What they carry comes after the #, which a browser never sends to a website: the page only hands it to the MeetPoint app on your phone.

Maps and search

Street maps come from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data). To download them, your phone asks OpenFreeMap's servers for the map around your meeting points, which gives them your IP address and those areas, as with any map. Searching for an address uses Apple's (iPhone) or Google's (Android) location services. The country for the emergency number is looked up on the phone, and checked with Apple's service on iPhone when there is internet.

Camera, Bluetooth and the log

The camera is used only to scan a family invite QR code; no picture is kept. Bluetooth is used for phones nearby and for your Meshtastic radio. The Log records what MeetPoint sent and received, and is kept only on your phone; it leaves only if you share it yourself.

This website

This website uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no embedded content from other sites; the fonts are served from this site too. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives your IP address as part of delivering the pages and may keep it in its server logs for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

MeetPoint is meant to be set up by a parent or guardian for the family. The developer collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

⚙︎ → Delete Plan removes the plan from your phone, and uninstalling MeetPoint removes everything it kept (plan, messages, log). Copies on your family's phones stay there until they delete them; sealed messages on the relay and on carrying phones expire on their own.
